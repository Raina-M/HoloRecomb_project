# Random fission and fusion simulation (CVCL)

`random_simulation.py` simulates chromosome breakage (fission-dominated) or fusion between pairs of karyotypes and compares the
coefficient of variation of chromosome length (CVCL) of the derived karyotype with the simulated distribution (Extended Data
Fig. 3). `random_simulation_results.txt` is the log of the runs for all species pairs (observed CVCL, simulated mean and s.d.,
z-score). Chromosome lengths of the species pairs are defined in the script.
